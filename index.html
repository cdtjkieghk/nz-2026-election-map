Map notes & sources
Method
The 2026 election uses 64 General electorates and 7 Māori electorates. Every location is covered by both a General electorate and a Māori electorate; electors vote on the roll on which they are enrolled. Toggle the boundary layer to avoid overlaying the two systems.
